Product asset management platform

A B2B AI platform that turns product photos into store-ready images, video and copy for retailers, with product information and digital asset management built in.
Role
Senior product designer
Scope
AI flows, asset editor, design system, marketing pages
Status
Live with US retailers

Crypto and everyday money wallet

A mobile app for holding crypto and handling everyday money, plus the admin console the operations team uses to run it.
Role
Product designer, research to handover
Scope
14 mobile flow groups, admin console, design system
Size
About 850 mobile screens
  • Scope first. Money, trust, business and operations mapped into 14 flow groups and agreed before design, while changes were still cheap. Each group was wireframed, then designed with empty, loading, error and success states.
  • One system. Colour, type and spacing tokens defined up front, with one component library shared by the app and the admin console.

My Design Process

How I go from a blank screen to a product people actually enjoy using.

Sarthak Jadvani Design Process
  1. Understanding the ProblemAsk questions first, to find the real problem behind the request.
  2. Defining User NeedsPersonas and journeys show what users need, ending in a clear problem statement.
  3. Brainstorming & IdeationSketch fast and wide, and explore several directions before choosing one.
  4. Wireframing & User FlowsLow-fidelity structure to test navigation, hierarchy and layout.
  5. Visual Design & UIHigh-fidelity screens in Figma: components, type and colour.
  6. PrototypingInteractive prototypes so the product can be felt before any code.
  7. Testing & RefiningTest with real users and iterate on usability, accessibility and clarity.
  8. Handoff & CollaborationDeveloper-ready files with specs and annotations, and support through launch.
